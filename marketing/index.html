Common questions
Are there any per-seat charges?
No. Add as many agents, admins, and viewers as you want on every plan — including free. We think it's absurd that most platforms charge you per person for a shared inbox.
What happens when I hit my message limit?
Your account keeps working — you just pay the per-message rate from the table above. If your wallet balance drops below the threshold you set, campaigns auto-pause until you top up (personal messages keep sending). No surprise bills.
Do I need to bring my own WhatsApp Business account?
Yes — Meta requires each business to hold its own WABA. We walk you through the setup in 10 minutes via our built-in onboarding wizard, and take care of the API-key exchange, phone number verification, and template submission.
Can I move existing conversations from another CPaaS?
Contact + template CSV import is one click on any plan. Full conversation history import needs a support session — we've done it for teams moving off Gupshup, Wati, and Interakt.
Is TalkEx DPDP-compliant?
Yes — consent records per purpose, one-click revoke-all, DSAR workflow, and Article 8-style processing register are built in. Data resides in Indian Postgres by default (Mumbai region). We are DPDP-2023 compliant out of the box.
Can I self-host TalkEx?
Enterprise plan includes on-premise / private-VPC deployment with a Docker Compose bundle. Every component (API, worker, Postgres, Redis) runs as a distroless container; single binary if you prefer no Docker.

01 · Quick start

Send your first message in three lines.

Generate an API key in Settings → Developers, then POST to /conversations/send. Every send routes through the same queue as campaign messages, so the priority, retry, and fallback logic applies for free.

  • Bearer auth. Same JWT the dashboard uses, or a long-lived API key.
  • Idempotency keys. Retry safely — no double-charge, no double-send.
  • Sub-200ms P95. Enqueue latency from the Mumbai region.
  • Rate limits in headers. X-RateLimit-{Limit,Remaining,Reset} on every response.
# Send a WhatsApp template to one contact
curl -X POST https://api.business.talkex.in/conversations/send \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $TALKEX_API_KEY" \
  -H "Idempotency-Key: order-4021-shipped" \
  -d '{
    "contact_id": "ct_saanvi_9845012345",
    "channel": "whatsapp",
    "template_id": "tpl_order_shipped",
    "variables": ["Saanvi", "4021"]
  }'

03 · What we believe

Four rules we haven't broken yet.

Software should feel calm.

The dashboard your agents open at 9am shouldn't flash red banners at them. Restraint in colour, restraint in animation, information ordered by what needs attention now — not by what's newest.

Fair pricing beats fair-sounding pricing.

Per-seat CPaaS is a tax on hiring agents. We charge for messages — the thing you're actually buying. Included allowances scale generously; overages pass through at actual provider cost.

Compliance is table stakes.

DPDP consent, DLT registration, Meta approval — these aren't premium features. They're the baseline for operating in India. Every plan includes the full compliance workflow.

Open standards over walled gardens.

OpenAPI, HMAC-signed webhooks, Postgres export, Docker deployment. Nothing about your data or workflow is locked to us — leave whenever you want, we'd rather you stay because it's good.

The total was ₹40,000 a month. Every conversation lived in its own silo. When a customer messaged on WhatsApp about an order and then followed up on Instagram, no agent could see both threads. When a campaign got scheduled in the marketing tool, the support inbox knew nothing about it — so when replies flooded in, agents were blindsided.

The dashboard that opened at 9am shouldn't flash red banners at you. That was the design brief for TalkEx, written on a whiteboard in a Koramangala WeWork on a Tuesday afternoon.

The three problems we set out to fix

Per-seat pricing is a tax on hiring agents. Every CPaaS we looked at charged between ₹800 and ₹2,500 per agent per month — so growing the support team meant a linear cost increase for tooling. That felt backwards. We charge for messages instead — the thing you're actually buying.

Compliance was treated as a premium feature. DPDP Act 2023 consent workflows, DLT registration for SMS, Meta template approval — these were "Enterprise plan" features on two of the three tools we shortlisted. For an Indian business, these aren't optional; they're the baseline for operating legally.

AI was bolted on. Every incumbent added a "Suggest reply" button somewhere between mid-2023 and 2024, and every implementation felt like a demo. The suggestions rarely made sense in context, and they weren't tied to the rest of the product.

What we built instead

TalkEx v1 shipped in November 2024 with WhatsApp Cloud API, a real wallet, and campaigns. v2 (what you see today) added seven more channels, a chatbot flow builder, Claude-powered AI Assist, and a live chat widget — all in eight months of small, boring commits.

We're a three-person team, we're self-funded, and we read every support email that comes in. If any of this resonates, try the free tier — it's 500 messages a month across all eight channels, no card required.

Market

The Indian CPaaS market in 2026.

02 Aug 2026Satya Manikanta12 min read

Three shifts changed the shape of Indian CPaaS in the last eighteen months. Any of them alone would have been notable; together they've reset the market.

1 · DPDP enforcement stopped being theoretical

The Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 passed with a lot of fanfare and then sat in a limbo of pending rules for eighteen months. In January 2026 the first enforcement notices went out — three D2C brands got hit with ₹50 lakh consent-violation fines for marketing WhatsApp sends without documented opt-in. Overnight, every CPaaS RFP started asking for consent workflow demos and DSAR handling.

2 · Meta's Cloud API killed the BSP monopoly

Until 2024, getting on WhatsApp Business meant going through a Business Solution Provider — Gupshup, Karix, Wati, Interakt — and their prices reflected the moat. Meta's direct Cloud API in India cut the BSP layer out. New CPaaS platforms could integrate directly at Meta's per-message rates. Legacy BSPs still exist for hand-holding onboarding but their margins compressed hard.

3 · DLT re-registration turned SMS into a burden

TRAI's second DLT overhaul in October 2025 forced every business to re-register every template. Response rates from Indian SMBs dropped by 40% in the first month. The businesses that stayed with SMS mostly shifted transactional traffic to WhatsApp utility templates.

All of this points at the same underlying trend: Indian businesses want one tool that handles all their messaging surfaces, respects local compliance, and doesn't cost like it was priced for the American market.

That's the market we're building for.

Migration

Migrating off Gupshup in a weekend.

18 Jul 2026Aditi Rao6 min read

We've helped a dozen teams switch CPaaS in the last six months. Here's the playbook that consistently works — start Friday evening, finish Sunday night, no production downtime.

Friday · export what you have

  1. Contacts CSV — export from your current tool with phone, name, tags, and any custom fields. Import into TalkEx via /contacts/import-csv — one click.
  2. Approved templates — screenshot each template's exact body and category. You'll re-submit these to Meta from TalkEx (approval takes ~24 hours).
  3. Team members — invite each agent to TalkEx via email. They can log in and browse while the migration finishes.

Saturday · re-submit templates + wire the API

  1. Create every template inside TalkEx and hit Submit to Meta. Approvals trickle in through Sunday.
  2. Point your webhook receivers at TalkEx (https://api.business.talkex.in/webhooks/channels/whatsapp). Keep the old webhook live too — dual-write for the transition.
  3. Wire your CRM's outbound sends to TalkEx's POST /conversations/send. If you're on Zapier / Make, we have pre-built connectors.

Sunday · cut over

  1. Once all templates are approved, flip Meta's webhook URL from the old provider to TalkEx.
  2. Freeze the old provider's outbound (pause campaigns, disable API keys).
  3. Monitor the TalkEx dashboard for the next 24 hours. Any misdirected inbound will show up as an "unknown contact" event — reroute in bulk from the UI.
The trick is dual-write during Saturday. If TalkEx catches fire, you can flip back to the old provider in five minutes.
